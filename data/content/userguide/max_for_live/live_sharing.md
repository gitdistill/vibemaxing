# Sharing Max for Live Devices
To create Live devices that you can give to other people, you'll need to ensure the device is packaged with all the files it needs. We call this [freezing](https://docs.cycling74.com/userguide/m4l/live_freezing/).
The use of the term _freezing_ here should not be confused with the Max concept of frozen attributes or frozen tracks in Ableton Live.
Basic freezing is explained [here](https://docs.cycling74.com/userguide/m4l/live_freezing/). If your device opens files during its operation (such as a folder of samples or images), you may need to add these files as dependencies before freezing. Once you freeze a device, it cannot be edited. You'll need to [unfreeze](https://docs.cycling74.com/userguide/m4l/live_unfreezing/) it first.
If you open a frozen device for editing and see this icon in the patcher toolbar
![](https://docs.cycling74.com/images/104afadda417dc6d2c972d4bfc150a07_47.webp)
you'll need to [resolve conflicts](https://docs.cycling74.com/userguide/m4l/live_resolveconflicts/) between the files in the frozen device and those you have on disk before you can unfreeze.
