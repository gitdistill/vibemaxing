# Tutorial 44: Flash Interactivity
In prior versions, Max gained access to Flash with the [jit.movie](https://docs.cycling74.com/reference/jit.movie "jit.movie") object. However, Apple has removed support for Flash media from QuickTime. Users wishing to work with Flash media in Max might look at the [jweb](https://docs.cycling74.com/reference/jweb/ "jweb") object, or 3rd party interface objects such as _FLOSC_. Alternatively, you might want to convert your Flash scripts to JavaScript, since the two languages are similar.
